Yaoki Reader is on Readings and meanings at a touch, on any Japanese page

On a phone or tablet: touch a Japanese word and keep your finger on it. Its reading appears over the kanji, and a card shows what each word means. Slide your finger to the next word; lift it and everything hides. A swipe still scrolls, a tap still follows a link.

With a mouse: hold the button down on a word, still, for a moment, or hold Shift and point. Esc puts the card away.

Try it on these lines

今日はいい天気ですね。公園まで歩きませんか。

駅前の古い本屋が、来月で店を閉じることになった。

雨が降っていたので、傘を持って図書館まで歩いた。

知らない言葉があっても、指で触れれば読み方と意味がわかる。

Nothing you read leaves this device. The dictionary is inside the extension, and it makes no network requests. The words you look up go on a list you can open from the toolbar button (and switch off there); on a phone, the toolbar button is in the browser's extensions menu. The first lookup after a while takes a few seconds while the dictionary loads.
