Yaoki Reader Readings and meanings at a touch

Touch a Japanese word and keep your finger on it. Its reading shows over the kanji and a card shows what each word means. Slide to the next word; lift your finger and it hides again.

With a mouse: hold the button down on a word, or hold Shift and point.

長押しの長さ · How long to hold

A shorter hold is quicker; a longer one leaves more time to start scrolling with a finger on the text.

文の訳は出ません。 A touch never shows a whole-sentence translation. You get the pieces and put the sentence together yourself. Everything is worked out on this device; nothing you read is sent anywhere.
