        Dictionary credits and limits

        Word meanings come from JMdict (2020 edition, with the words and spellings added up to the 2026 edition), the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used in conformance with the Group's licence (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike). The shortened word list this page loads is derived from it and is offered under the same licence. A kanji no word explains shows its own meaning, from KANJIDIC2 (the same Group, same licence), marked “(kanji)”.

        Word splitting and readings use kuromoji.js (Apache-2.0) with the IPA dictionary from the mecab-ipadic project. That dictionary predates 2019, so for words it only knows in pieces (令和, 一本, 八日) the reading is taken from JMdict instead.

        Typefaces: Noto Serif JP (print) and Klee One (textbook), both under the SIL Open Font License.

        Names. A name the dictionary does not have is shown with its reading in roman letters and “(probably a name)”, or “(foreign name or word)” in katakana. In works from Aozora Bunko the author's own furigana give the reading of names and rare words, and of words with several readings.

        Limits. Everything is worked out by rule on your device, with no person checking it. Readings can be wrong where a word has several (降る and 降りる, 今日, names without furigana). Meanings show the first common sense of a word, which is not always the one the sentence uses. Grammar notes cover about ninety common patterns. Older spelling (旧仮名遣い: ゐる, 思ふ, 帰つて) is mostly understood, but less well than modern spelling.

        Aozora Bunko (青空文庫, aozora.gr.jp) is a library of Japanese works whose copyright has ended, typed in and checked by volunteers. The list of works this page searches is made from Aozora Bunko's own index and holds only works out of copyright. The texts are fetched from aozorahack's copy of Aozora Bunko's files on GitHub, since Aozora Bunko's own site cannot be read by other pages; works added or corrected since that copy was last updated can be loaded from the file Aozora Bunko offers for download. Ruby (furigana) and notes in Aozora's text format are taken out, so the readings you see are this page's own.
